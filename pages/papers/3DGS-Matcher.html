One representation, many possibilities.

3DGS-Matcher uses the rich appearance and geometry encoded by 3D Gaussian Splatting to establish reliable matches across views and scenes.

Our method is designed to be practical: it works with existing reconstructions, scales to large scenes, and makes correspondences easy to inspect.
